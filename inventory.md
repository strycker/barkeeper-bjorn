## Veto Lists

### Disliked Ingredients (Never Suggest)

> **Permanent vetoes.** Never propose cocktails containing these ingredients. Do not suggest as purchases.

- **Chartreuse** (yellow or green) — not Glenn's style. Excludes Last Word, Bijou, Naked & Famous, Final Ward, etc.
- **Egg** — no egg-based cocktails. Excludes flips, egg-white sours (Pisco Sour, Clover Club, Boston Sour, Whiskey Sour with foam, Ramos Gin Fizz, etc.), and any whole-egg or egg-white build.

### Substitute For Now (Will Buy Eventually)

> **Temporary substitutions.** Recipes calling for these get presented normally with the sub applied.

- **Tequila** → substitute **mezcal 1:1**. Glenn enjoys tequila and plans to add it eventually. For citrus-forward shaken drinks (Margarita, Sidecar variants), the mezcal sub is excellent. For refresher-style cocktails (Paloma, Tommy's Margarita), the mezcal sub changes character meaningfully — flag the trade-off when relevant.
